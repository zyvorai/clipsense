# Trial and licence

- **No key:** everything works; the status line says so.
- **Trial key:** the status line shows the days left.
- **Expired or invalid key:** the status line says so and the app still works.
- No feature is ever disabled, hidden or degraded by the licence state.

The app only verifies a key on your Mac against a built-in public key. It cannot create keys and sends nothing anywhere.
